English

Trail is an offline puzzle game by Farcrak Labs. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

  • We do not collect personal data. The app has no account, no sign-in and does not request internet access. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Data on your device. Your progress, stars, outfit and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • No ads, purchases or analytics. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Sharing. If you tap “Share” on a result, Android’s share sheet opens; you choose the app and recipients yourself. We do not receive what you share.
  • Google Play. Google Play may process data when you download the app or take part in testing, under Google’s Privacy Policy. Feedback you send us by email is used only to answer you and improve the game.
  • Children. The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

«Тропа» — офлайн-головоломка студии Farcrak Labs. Здесь объяснено, что происходит с вашими данными во время игры.

  • Мы не собираем персональные данные. В приложении нет аккаунта и входа, оно не запрашивает доступ к интернету. Ничего из того, что вы делаете в игре, не отправляется ни нам, ни кому-либо ещё.
